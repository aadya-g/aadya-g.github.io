# 👋 About Me
Hi, I’m **Aadya**.  
I’m currently an undergraduate student at the **University of Wisconsin–Madison** studying **Computer Science** and **Data Science**.

My main interests include:

- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** exploring how they learn, adapt, and can be fine-tuned for real-world applications.
- **Computer Vision (CV):** using deep learning for image recognition, object detection, and visual understanding.
- **Multimodal AI:** exploring how models can combine information from different modalities, such as text, images, and video, to better understand and interact with the world.
- **Reinforcement Learning (RL):** studying how intelligent agents can learn through interaction, feedback, and trial and error.

I grew up in the Bay Area, surrounded by innovation and creativity, which sparked my curiosity about technology from an early age. That curiosity has led me to pursue Computer Science and Data Science at UW-Madison, where I’ve had the chance to explore a wide range of projects and ideas.  

Research and interdisciplinary learning opportunities have been a hallmark of my experience, whether it’s building AI tools to simplify real-world tasks, analyzing data to uncover patterns, or experimenting with web projects that combine design and functionality.  

👉 Check out the [Projects](/projects/) page to see what I’ve been working on.
