## Undergraduate Research Assistant

*May 2026 - Present*

My work focuses on improving how multimodal models interpret surgical procedures, including identifying instruments, anatomy, surgical phases, and procedural steps.

I build data preprocessing pipelines in **Python** to transform raw surgical video annotations into structured VQA datasets for model training and evaluation. One of the datasets I worked with contains **8,800+ question-answer pairs** spanning instrument, anatomy, phase, and step categories.

I also run **QLoRA fine-tuning experiments on 72B-parameter VLMs** using **PyTorch and Hugging Face Transformers**. I evaluate model performance across different surgical reasoning tasks and compare results against other vision-language models to understand where different approaches perform well and where they struggle. 

Through this work, I’ve gained experience working with large-scale multimodal models, GPU/HPC computing environments, model fine-tuning, dataset development, and evaluation of AI systems in a specialized domain.
